# Claims

- Mechanism claim: Cross embodiment force vocabularies keeps action-critical alternatives explicit until a physical observation collapses them.
- Evidence claim: the included real MuJoCo benchmark fits force/effect tokens from source embodiments and evaluates held-out robot embodiments, friction/object shifts, robust MPC, raw-force transfer, ablations, and oracle upper bounds.
- Scope claim: results support mechanism plausibility and weak-baseline gains, but not ICLR-main readiness or real-robot deployment.
- Unsupported claim explicitly avoided: no claim of SOTA robot performance.
- Terminal claim: STRONG_REVISE, because robust domain-randomized MPC ties or beats CEFV on important splits and ablations do not yet prove all proposed components are necessary.
